1. Section 3, Proposition 1 and its sketch (also Section 2 item 2). The sandwich e_lat <= e_0 <= e_H is false as stated for the Hamiltonian with periodised kernel, H = -1/2 Delta + sum_{i<j} U_per(x_i - x_j). The theorem, TorusBound.lean (`pointEnergy`: "interaction of x_i with all other points of C", own images included; verifier report Sec. 5 "images x_i+m, m != 0, are included"), LEDGER CLAIM-114 ("all images, including a point's own") and your own Section 6.2 bound the energy of the periodic extension, which contains the self-image energy c_L = (1/2) sum_{m in L\0} U(m). The sketch uses the no-self-image energy for the upper bound ((1-1/N)) and the with-self-image energy for the lower bound. Counterexample N = 1: no interaction, e_0 = 0 < e_lat(rho). The numerics are right (they include the self-images); the proposition is not. Replacement: "For N bosons on R^2/L with covol(L) rho = N and H = -1/2 Delta + sum_{i<j} U_per, and c_L = (1/2) sum_{m in L\0} U(m) the interaction of a particle with its own images: e_lat(rho) - c_L <= e_0(rho) <= (1/2) rho U~(0) (1-1/N) <= e_H(rho). If N c_L is added to H (the energy of the periodic extension, which is what the theorem and torus_lower_bound bound and Section 6 computes) the lower bound reads e_lat(rho) <= e_0. For kernels decaying faster than r^-2, c_L -> 0 as N -> infinity at fixed rho, so Table 1 is unaffected." In the sketch: "the theorem bounds the energy of its periodic extension, self-images included, by e_lat(rho)".
