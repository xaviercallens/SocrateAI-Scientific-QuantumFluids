## 0. The book in one paragraph

A textbook that teaches quantum-fluid physics through three instruments used together: **(i) the experiment** — the measurements of Henri Godfrin and his collaborators (and a few other classic experiments), **(ii) the proof assistant** — the Lean 4 library `QuantumFluids` (≈310 machine-checked theorems; sources in `lean_src/`, index in `facts/lean_index.md`, compile audit in `facts/lean_audit.md` when finished), **(iii) the solver** — rusty-SUNDIALS (CVODE through the Python module `rusty_sundials`, and the quantum-fluids crate `qf-pgpe` through the Python extension `qf_pgpe`). It is a **tribute**: to the experimental craft of Henri Godfrin (neutron scattering on helium films, microkelvin physics), told honestly. Ten chapters, written separately and combined by the editor (me). You write ONE chapter.

Audience: graduate students and curious experimentalists/mathematicians. Tone: clear, warm, exact, never promotional. Admiration is expressed by *explaining why the measurements are hard and what they decided*, not by adjectives.

## 2. Verified facts about Henri Godfrin's work (the only biography allowed)

(Verified 2026-10-09 from the Institut Néel page, the Nature page listing, the neutron-sources news item, and the programme's literature ledger LIT-001/002.)

* **Name:** Henri Godfrin (the repository's older files write "Henry"; use *Henri*). CNRS Director of Research at the **Institut Néel, Grenoble**; permanent researcher of the *Ultra-low Temperature group* (UBT), which does condensed-matter experiments down to the **microkelvin** range: nuclear adiabatic-demagnetization cryostats reaching about 100 µK, dilution refrigerators with 8 mK base temperature, **neutron scattering** for the structure and excitations of quantum matter, quantum fluids and solids (³He, ⁴He), amorphous matter, microwave optomechanics; part of the European Microkelvin Platform.
* **Nature 483, 576–579 (2012):** H. Godfrin, M. Meschke, H.-J. Lauter, A. Sultan, H. M. Böhm, E. Krotscheck, M. Panholzer, *Observation of a roton collective mode in a two-dimensional Fermi liquid.* Inelastic neutron scattering at the Institut Laue–Langevin (ILL, Grenoble) on **two-dimensional liquid ³He**. EDITOR CORRECTION 2026-10-10 from the authors' copy (HAL hal-00920422): the sample is **a monolayer** of liquid ³He (areal density 4.7±0.2 atoms/nm²) on ZYX exfoliated graphite pre-plated with a solid ⁴He monolayer, measured on the time-of-flight spectrometer **IN6** — NOT "a few atomic layers". The paper's claim: the collective density mode, strongly damped inside the particle–hole band, reappears at momentum transfers larger than twice the Fermi momentum as a well-defined excitation with a roton-like (phonon–maxon–roton) dispersion, "unexpected collective behaviour" outside the scope of Landau's theory. Do NOT write "far longer lived in 2D than in the bulk" (a news-item phrase, not in the paper). Institutions: CNRS (France), Aalto University, Oak Ridge National Laboratory, SUNY Buffalo, Johannes Kepler University Linz. Published 29 March 2012.
* **Phys. Rev. B 103, 104516 (2021)** (arXiv:2012.09067): H. Godfrin, K. Beauvois, A. Sultan, E. Krotscheck, J. Dawidowski, B. Fåk, J. Ollivier (EDITOR CORRECTION 2026-10-10, checked against Crossref for doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.103.104516: the second author is **K.** Beauvois, not "M."; there is no Sokol among the authors; published 29 March 2021), inelastic neutron scattering on superfluid ⁴He at the ILL spectrometer **IN5**, the dispersion relation of the Landau elementary excitations (phonon–maxon–roton), and the thermodynamic consequences. (EDITOR CORRECTION: do NOT write "about 50 points" — the authors' ancillary dispersion table `data/external/godfrin_2021_arxiv_ancillary/DispersionP0allRange.txt` has 1727 rows from 0 to 3.6 Å⁻¹, 34 of them with an uncertainty; it is author-processed, not raw data.) **Eq. (22)** gives the phonon specific heat `C_V = A T³ + C T⁵ + D T⁶ + E T⁷ + K T⁸ + L T⁹` for a dispersion `ε = ħck(1 + α₂k² + … + α₆k⁶)` with `α₁ = 0`; the paper remarks that earlier published versions of this series contain errors.
* **Review:** H. Godfrin and E. Krotscheck, *The Dynamics of Quantum Fluids*, arXiv:2206.06039 (2022): a survey of excitation spectra of quantum fluids (phonon–roton, zero sound, Landau theory).
* **J. Low Temp. Phys. (2012)**, *Static structure factor of two-dimensional liquid ³He adsorbed on graphite* (Springer, doi:10.1007/s10909-012-0649-9) — EDITOR 2026-10-10, checked with Crossref: A. Sultan, M. Meschke, H.-J. Lauter, H. Godfrin, J. Low Temp. Phys. 169(5–6), 367–376 (2012); first author Sultan, so write "Sultan et al."; cite only as "same programme of 2D ³He measurements".
* **What this library has done with that work:** (a) Eq. (22) of PRB 103, 104516 was **independently derived (sympy) and proved in Lean** — all six coefficients A, C, D, E, K, L and the inverse series with η; *a confirmation, not a finding* (`BoseIntegral`, `PhononSeries`, `PhononSpecificHeat`; history in `docs/designs/PHONON_SERIES_A_TO_L.md`). (b) `HeliumKinematics` formalizes kinematic identities stated in that paper's analysis of neutron data (three-phonon process threshold, symmetric split of the dispersion, time-of-flight energy formulas eq. 12 = eq. 13, Landau velocity, Debye mode count). (c) `ZeroSound` proves, in 2D and 3D, that an undamped zero-sound root exists iff the Landau parameter F > 0, with the explicit 2D root `s = (1+F)/√(1+2F)`; the 2D case is included *because* the 2012 measurement is on a 2D film.
* **What is NOT established and must not be claimed:** that the 2D ³He Landau parameters of the 2012 experiment are known to us (no open numeric table was found); anything about the data-availability of the raw ILL data; any statement about errata in the 2021 paper (the programme's check of Eq. (22) found none). Do not discuss the unresolved items in `docs/FOR_GODFRIN.md`.
