## 2. Binding lessons of the first edition (all were real defects)

1. **Lean citations.** `\Lthm{Module}{name}` with Module = the FILE name in `lean_src/` (it prints the namespace actually
   declared; 13 modules are not `QuantumFluids.<Module>`, table `facts/lean_namespaces.md`). Names may contain plain
   `_`. New Lean for the book: `book/lean/ChNN_<Name>.lean`, `namespace QuantumFluids.<Name>`, cite as
   `\Lthm{<Name>}{thm}`; zero errors, no `sorry`, `#print axioms` lines at the end; a negative control is welcome but
   must be a separate file named `ChNN_NegativeControl_*.lean` whose header says it is MEANT TO FAIL.
2. **Listings.** `qfbook.sty` makes non-ASCII characters in `lstlisting` print in order, but only those listed in
   `qf_unicode_lst.tex`. After writing your listings run
   `flock /mnt/data/xdev-cache/tmp/qf_heavy.lock python3 facts/make_appA.py` (regenerates that file, Appendix A and the
   citation check `facts/appA_citation_check.md`, which must report 0 problems for your chapter), then recompile and
   LOOK at every listing in the PDF.
3. **Figures.** `figstyle.py` gives true minus signs and lining digits for single-line text; multi-line `Text` and
   mathtext fragments keep old-style digits, so prefer one-line labels. Every figure needs a short caption for the List
   of Figures: `\caption[Short title]{Long caption ...}`. ≥ 3 figures, one striking.
4. **CVODE.** The `.venv` module `rusty_sundials` is STALE (first-order Adams). Run with
   `PYTHONPATH=/mnt/data/xdev-cache/rs_py_5db8041:/mnt/data/xdev-cache/qf_ext .venv/bin/python`, assert the Adams probe
   (y'=−y to t=10, rtol 1e-8, Adams needs < 5000 RHS calls), record module path, sha256 and commit 5db8041 in your
   numbers JSON. `CvodeSolver.solve` cannot integrate backwards in time.
5. **Godfrin.** Only FACTS §2 as corrected: the 2012 sample is a MONOLAYER of liquid ³He on ⁴He-preplated graphite,
   measured on IN6 (the 2021 ⁴He work on IN5); authors "K. Beauvois"; JLTP 2012 is "Sultan et al."; never "a few atomic
   layers", never "far longer lived in 2D than in the bulk". Nothing personal, no contact, nothing from
   `docs/FOR_GODFRIN.md`. His group's microkelvin techniques may be described as in §2.
6. **Citations.** Check every new reference with Crossref (`https://api.crossref.org/works/<doi>` or
   `query.bibliographic`) or arXiv; put them in `refs_chNN.bib`; brace isotopes and proper nouns in titles
   (`{$^3$He}`, `{Bose}`); list in your report how each was checked.
7. **Environment.** `rm`/`mv` are denied: build with a private wrapper `chapter_wrapper_chNN.tex` (top-level wrappers and
   PDFs are git-ignored) and a fresh `-jobname`; never redirect console output to `<job>.out`. Heavy jobs (> 1 min) under
   `flock /mnt/data/xdev-cache/tmp/qf_heavy.lock nice ...`; release the lock between steps.

## 3. The new chapters (anchors are suggestions to verify, not facts to copy)

Each follows FACTS §6 (opening from a measurement, physics, 2–5 real Lean statements with an abridged proof idea, at least
one rusty-SUNDIALS computation compared quantitatively, ≥ 3 figures, honest box, three exercises with hints, notes and
further reading; 14–20 pages) AND writes `chapters/solNN.tex` with worked solutions of its three exercises (§4).

* **ch11 — Bose–Einstein condensation.** Physics: ideal Bose gas (critical density nλ³ = ζ(3/2), condensate fraction,
  harmonic trap T_c and 1 − (T/T_c)³), interactions and the Gross–Pitaevskii energy functional, Thomas–Fermi limit,
  healing length, Penrose–Onsager off-diagonal long-range order, why 2D is different (Mermin–Wagner, quasi-condensate;
  link to \cref{ch06}). Anchors to verify: Einstein 1924/25, London 1938, Penrose & Onsager 1956, Anderson et al. Science
  1995, Davis et al. PRL 1995. Lean ideas: `BoseIntegral` already has `hasSum_mellin_bose` (Re s > 1) → the Bose function
  at s = 3/2 and 3; algebra of the condensate fraction; properties of the Thomas–Fermi profile. Solver ideas: exact
  finite-N Bose counting in a 3D harmonic trap vs the thermodynamic limit (finite-size shift of T_c); imaginary-time
  ground state (`qf-gpe2d` supports imaginary time; or CVODE on a normalized gradient flow in 1D/2D) vs the Thomas–Fermi
  chemical potential and radius. Optional data: Sunami et al. Science 382, 443 (2023), `data/external/sunami2023_zenodo_8385524` (CC BY 4.0).
* **ch12 — Two-fluid hydrodynamics and second sound.** Physics: Tisza/Landau two-fluid model, normal-fluid density from
  the excitation spectrum (Landau's formula), first and second sound (the biquadratic for the two speeds), fourth sound,
  2D second sound across the BKT transition. Data: the authors' dispersion table of Godfrin et al. 2021 (ρ_n(T), s(T),
  c₂(T) from the measured spectrum; compare with the paper's thermodynamic results) and Christodoulou et al., Nature 594,
  191 (2021), `data/external/christodoulou2021_apollo_CAM66056` (CC BY 4.0; first and second sound in a 2D BKT superfluid).
  Programme history to tell: LEDGER CLAIM-043, prediction P6 (part 1 PASS, part 2 FAIL as written). Lean ideas: root
  structure of the two-fluid biquadratic; the phonon ρ_n ∝ T⁴ coefficient via the Bose integrals. Solver: CVODE
  quadratures of Landau's integrals over the measured dispersion.
* **ch13 — Giant vortex clusters and Onsager's negative temperatures.** Physics: Kirchhoff point-vortex dynamics and its
  invariants, Onsager's statistical hydrodynamics (bounded phase space → negative temperature, like-sign clustering),
  evaporative heating. Data: Gauthier et al., Science 364, 1264 (2019), `data/external/gauthier2019_zenodo_2548958` (CC BY
  4.0; experimental vortex positions); anchor to verify: Johnstone et al., Science 364, 1267 (2019); Onsager 1949.
  Programme history to tell: CLAIM-043 P5 (decay-time ratio 2.2 vs threshold 3: FAIL as written); CLAIM-069 (the
  programme's anomalous PGPE states are NOT Onsager clusters: refuted, `docs/designs/PGPE_ONSAGER_RESULTS.md`). Lean
  ideas: conservation of energy and impulse for Kirchhoff's equations; existing `MatchingScreening`, `PairPolarisation`.
  Solver: CVODE N-vortex dynamics (disc with image vortices, or doubly periodic), clustering measures compared with the
  experimental positions.
* **ch14 — Fermi superfluids, BCS and superfluid ³He.** Physics: Cooper instability, BCS gap equation, Δ(T), the
  universal ratios 2Δ(0)/k_BT_c = 2π e^{−γ} ≈ 3.53 and ΔC/C_n = 12/(7ζ(3)) ≈ 1.43, p-wave pairing and the A and B phases
  of ³He, the Landau Fermi liquid as the normal state (link to \cref{ch08}), the microkelvin techniques that make ³He
  superfluidity observable (FACTS §2 describes the Grenoble group's). Anchors to verify: BCS 1957, Osheroff–Richardson–Lee
  1972 and Osheroff–Gully–Richardson–Lee 1972, Leggett RMP 1975, Vollhardt & Wölfle (book). Do NOT attribute ³He
  superfluidity results to Godfrin. Lean ideas: monotonicity of the gap integral in Δ (uniqueness of the gap), algebraic
  weak-coupling relations, definitions and checked numeric bounds of the universal constants if feasible. Solver: CVODE
  or quadrature for Δ(T), the specific-heat jump, the ratios against their closed forms.
* **ch15 — Quantum turbulence.** Physics: Feynman's tangle, Vinen and Kolmogorov regimes, Kelvin waves, the energy
  cascade, shell models as minimal cascades, incompressible kinetic-energy spectra of GP fields. Anchors to verify:
  Feynman 1955, Vinen 1957, Maurer & Tabeling EPL 1998, Navon et al. Nature 539, 72 (2016), Barenghi–Skrbek–Sreenivasan
  PNAS 2014. Data: `data/external/polanco_2021_gp_turbulence` (check its licence/meta file). Lean: the library modules
  `ShellHamiltonian`, `QuantumFluidsShell`, `SigmaRule` (currently cited by no chapter) and any new statement. Solver: the
  `qf-shell-cascade` crate of rusty-SUNDIALS (`/home/xavkal/xdev/rusty-SUNDIALS-c3/crates/qf-shell-cascade`, README;
  build with `CARGO_TARGET_DIR` under `/mnt/data/xdev-cache/`) and/or CVODE on a shell model; spectra and fluxes.
  Programme history: check `LEDGER.md` and `docs/designs/M2_W4_DISPERSIVE_SHELL.md` for the shell-model claims.
